Privacy

Last updated 10 November 2026

What we collect

When you fill in the application form we collect your name, Instagram handle, email address, phone number, your main role and your answer to the investment question. If you book a call, Calendly also collects the details you enter there.

Your answers are saved as you go through the form, so we may receive them even if you don't finish it.

Why we collect it

Only to review your application, contact you about it and run the call you booked. We don't sell your information or use it for anything else.

Where it goes

  • Google Sheets (Google): where your form answers are stored.
  • Calendly: if you book a call.
  • Wistia: plays the videos on this site and may record viewing data.

The site sets no cookies of its own. Your browser keeps your form answers for the current visit only, to fill in the booking page for you.

Deleting your information

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, send a DM to @erikreborn1 on Instagram and we'll take care of it.
